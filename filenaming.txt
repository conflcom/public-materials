What do the files mean:

WordMark: Only the stylized text of the logo
Icon - Only the icon from the logo(In our case its a circle with two circles, kind of mimicking a face)
LockUp - The text and the icon together.

Bow - Black on white(Black text, white backround)
Wow - White on black(White text, black backround)

The two main colors used here are:

White - D5D2CC
Black - 212020
